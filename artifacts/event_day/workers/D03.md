## Calibration

The deployment parameters were measured against the live runs and left as they were. `timeout_seconds: 120` has
2.7× headroom over the worst call (45 s, contended). `max_tokens: 1024` produced no truncation or schema failures.
`context_tokens` equals the served `max_model_len`, and 12 frames at 768 px gives a 2–9 s inspect without
contention. The only latency problem was contention on the **shared** Qwen server, from other sessions' requests.
The fix for that is operational: keep other model jobs off the GPU while the judged run records. It is not a
profile change, and the server was not restarted.
